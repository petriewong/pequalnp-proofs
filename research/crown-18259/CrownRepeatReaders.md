# Three pure pairs with repeated raw readers: 34 to 11 necessary profiles

Scope: a normalized monotone binary 22-gate Crown7 circuit with exactly three pure matched ORs. A pure matched OR is the only consumer of both corresponding raw inputs. Among four split labels and two sides, type O is a sole OR reader, A is a sole AND with one downstream OR, and R means two OR readers. These follow from the two-gate zero-shadow bound. The previous clean-feeder argument excludes fewer than three non-O raws; this branch assumes at least one R. A differently labeled opposite A–A pair is impossible since either A needs the opposite raw singleton to activate its companion, but that opposite raw's sole AND is zero on its singleton.

## New exact structural exclusions

The three pure gates disappear when identifying twins. Deleting one raw on each side at distinct labels preserves seven-variable threshold two after identification, with a known floor of 16. Thus outside the pure prefix at most three physical gates can be eliminated.

* For mismatched opposite R–A raws x,y, each zero-shadow has two gates and they must intersect. The first A reader is an AND, so the intersection must be the sole downstream OR of that AND, which is also an OR directly reading x. Consequently x=1 masks every effect of y on the output, but singleton x is false and the promised pair x,y is true. This excludes every mismatched R–A type pair.
* For mismatched R–R, their shadows must share a raw-raw OR. Sharing both would mask the positive pair just as above (and duplicate the original gate). For mismatched R–O their shadows are disjoint: a shared OR would mask the sole-reader O raw. For A–O, if the sole-reader O gate were A's downstream OR, setting O high would mask A. Thus any surviving cut on two mismatched opposite raws involving R or A loses exactly three gates outside the prefix.
* Two split labels i,j both of type RR force reciprocal raw ORs x_i OR y_j and x_j OR y_i. On a cut through the two other split labels both gates survive the raw part but become identical after identifying twins. If either removed input is R or A, the cut already had the six allowed losses, so that duplicate is impossible. Hence both other labels must be OO. Their own opposite cuts also lose six (three prefix, two direct readers, one reciprocal alias).

Enumerating the 6,561 type assignments and canonicalizing by matched-label permutations and side swap yields 34 to 17 to 15. These are necessary profiles, not counts of realizable circuits. No statement is made about circuits with fewer pure pairs or all ordinary size-22 circuits. Exact positive witness remains 23.

## Matched-time benchmark

Run python3 repeat_profiles.py for the categorical count. The two one-repeat priorities are AO,AO,AO,RO and AO,AO,OO,RO. priority.py accepts baseline or guarded, the type string without commas, and a wall-time budget in seconds. The guarded version adds the three opposite saturated cuts deleting the sole repeated raw and one of the three compatible opposing inputs. In the cut signatures, at least 16 distinct projected gates and at least ten ORs must survive. The baseline and guarded modes use identical promised-row, topology and gate constraints, bit-blast tactic, and budget. A timeout is unknown; SAT is independently checked on all 49 promised rows.

## Clean-reader erasure gives four more exclusions (15 to 11)

Call a gate clean when it rejects every original matched diagonal. In every one of the 15 profiles, each label i has a saturated opposite-side cut chosen away from it: at least three exceptional inputs imply at least two exceptional labels, so pick an exceptional raw on another label and an opposite raw whose index differs from both. That cut loses six gates exactly.

For a sole OR G reading v at i, its companion rejects every different diagonal: otherwise turning v on there cannot change the result through its sole reader, but creates a promised off-diagonal by monotonicity. G cannot directly read a second raw of a different label by the same masking argument. Hence G cannot be a directly lost outside-label reader, nor an outside-label A shadow's downstream OR (that also masks the outside A at the diagonal of v). It survives the chosen cut. If its companion accepts its own diagonal, on the retained tied singleton z_i the companion is true, so by monotonicity G aliases that companion on the entire identified cube, as a forbidden seventh loss. Thus every O feeder has a clean companion.

For an A raw x_i, its sole reader H=x_i AND V rejects every diagonal except possibly its own. H cannot directly read another raw of a different label. If that input is on the same side it masks x_i on its promised positives; if it is opposite it masks x_i on another opposite index. It also cannot be another A's downstream OR. Thus H is not among the six certified losses away from i. If H accepts its tied singleton, it aliases the raw z_i, a forbidden further loss. Therefore every A reader is clean.

Every A's downstream OR K consumes its H. K cannot directly read a raw of another label, as that raw's diagonal would mask x_i's influence through K. K cannot directly read its twin either: in the saturated cut away from i, K would then survive but become z_i OR (z_i AND V)=z_i. (Such a K cannot also be a second A's downstream OR since its other input would already be occupied.) Thus the K gates are distinct from all O feeders and from the pure prefix. Distinct A raws have distinct H gates: a shared sole AND cannot support their promised positives. Each K can consume at most two distinct Hs.

Erase internal reads of clean wires and collect the surviving clean AND terms. Each O feeder and each distinct K collapses, without eliminating the sole pure-pair conduits of essential inputs. As soon as O + ceil(A/2) is at least six, this buys at least six deletions at the cost of at most five terminal ORs (the original has at most six ANDs). The resulting circuit has at most 21 gates, previously excluded for three or more pure pairs. This eliminates AO,AO,AO,RO and AO,AO,OO,RO (both one-repeat priorities) and also AO,OO,RO,RO and AR,OO,OO,RO (two repeats). Eleven necessary profiles remain in this branch. All eight original priority cases are now structurally excluded. The broader raw 22 question is still open.
