# Narrowing three-pair Crown circuits with unique raw readers

This is a scoped result, not the full raw Crown minimum. Fix the seven-index graph, and assume exactly three pure pair gates: a pure pair is the sole use of its two matched raw variables and is an OR. Suppose every raw variable has one reader. The argument below narrows the remaining normalized footprints; the residual fifth-term cases are not yet excluded.

## Normalize and count

The preparatory argument in CrownCleanFeederBound.md gives the only possible (m,A) values: (2,7), (3,6), (3,7), (4,5), (4,6), (4,7), where m are the split raw inputs read by AND and A is all AND gates. All 8−m singleton OR readers have clean companions, and all m AND readers are on one side except for a possible pair of opposite readers of the same label. A clean signal is false on every matched diagonal.

The m split raw AND readers are themselves clean. If two are a matched pair, their own diagonal starts with only their two inputs. The first of the two AND feeders cannot respond without the later twin having reached the network; after it is zero the second cannot respond either. Otherwise we can choose, for any given AND reader of label i, an AND raw of another label a and a compatible opposite-side sole OR labelled b, with b distinct from a and i. As in CrownCleanFeederBound.md, one of two OR choices is disjoint from the AND's two-gate zero-shadow. After deleting a and b, three pure ORs and the two-plus-one zero-shadow gates exhaust the drop budget. Keep x_i,y_i tied. If the reader for label i were true on its own singleton, it would become the identity z_i after projection: it is of form z_i AND H, with H>=z_i by monotonicity. This is a forbidden extra deletion. So all m split AND readers are clean.

Erase reads of every originally clean gate and collect the clean AND terms at the output. Let q be the surviving productive clean terms. Each of the 8−m clean-companion ORs disappears and terminal collection costs q−1 ORs; if q<9−m this would yield at most 21 gates with three pure pairs, previously excluded. At least m split raw AND terms survive, since their raw signals remain essential and have no other readers. With OR-only routing there must be at least two further clean products to cover the three pure variables: on these axes a sole product of two disjunctions with disjoint singleton supports cannot cover all three pairs. Thus q>=m+2 when routing is OR-only. With a dirty helper this triangle bound needs a separate check and is not assumed.

The m AND terms each have one split raw as their only occurrence, and must accept the off-diagonals incident to that raw. Their other operands are **star supports**: on singleton inputs they must contain all three pure atoms and the appropriate opposite split atoms other than their own twin. They omit their own twin, since their term is clean. This remains true *after* erasure: on an edge using a uniquely read raw, if no retained term uses that raw, the collection would already be true on an opposing primary alone. Thus each corresponding star term still witnesses all those positives.

At most 11+m−A routing ORs remain outside the three pure gates (22 less A, three, and 8−m). At most A−q dirty ANDs can appear in that routing. The added terminal ORs are not part of the star routing.

## Support-routing checks

A wire in the one-hot singleton layer can be represented by a set of true source positions. AND gives intersection, OR gives union. Set irrelevant extra split atoms to zero and regard the three pure flags as source atoms. For m star labels on a single side the respective outputs on seven atoms are all-but-one masks, with three mandatory common pure atoms and m withheld positions among four split atoms.

The checked pure-OR minimum gate counts for m=2,3,4 are 6,8,10. Exact backwards union replay is star-union-floor.py; the pure lower finite predicates for 3@7 and 4@9 also compile in Lean. The corresponding necessary mixed-support models with exactly one AND (zero and copies allowed as padding) are empty for 3@8 and 4@9. The Z3 bit-blast checker and independent minimal-network shards are recorded; the Lean generator lists the companion bounded cases. Only explicit checked cases are used, not timed-out circuit runs.

* m=2, A=7: q is seven, so only OR routing, at most six ORs. If the two AND readers are matched, their support union already requires nine distinct source atoms (three common, three split-left, three split-right). The union of their ancestor networks is connected through any common source, so six binary gates cannot connect all nine leaves (the count requires at least eight). If both readers are on one side, the exact minimum six ORs are spent entirely reaching their two star supports. No irrelevant extra split-left atom can be read in those ancestors: setting it zero would drop at least one of the six ORs without changing the necessary supports. No other routing gate is available. The two leftover split-left raw inputs must therefore appear directly in clean terminal factors. Each such row needs two terms to cover the two withheld columns because no ancestor of either star contains both those fringe atoms; a term containing only that row raw and one such factor cannot cover both. Distinct leftover rows cannot share those direct-occurrence terms. That needs at least 2+4+2=8 clean terms including pure-axis restriction, impossible at seven.
* m=3, A=6: all six terms clean, all eight routing ORs spent on three star supports. As before the fourth split row has no routing and needs two terms to cover the three withheld columns (no ancestor of a star covers all three). It needs m+2+2=7 terms, impossible at six.
* m=3, A=7: q>=6 and at most one dirty AND, at most seven routing ORs. No star network (OR-only minimum eight; mixed 3@8 empty).
* m=4, A=5: q=5 and no dirty gate; one remaining clean term cannot cover the pure triangle.
* m=4, A=6 or 7: if q>=6, the A=6 case has only nine ORs, and the A=7 case at most eight ORs and one AND. Both are excluded by the support checks. If q=5, A=6 has at most one dirty helper and A=7 at most two. The alternative q=5 must be checked as explained below.

## Status of the fifth-term exception

The pure-triangle argument requires care if a remaining term can read a dirty helper. A clean AND of arbitrary monotone antecedents need not by itself require two products on a pure triangle. Accordingly the claims above use q>=m+2 directly only when the routing is OR-only, or when q>=m+2 already follows from the erasure budget. For m=4, the remaining q=5, A=6 or 7 cases require exact checking with the possibility of dirty helpers. For m=3, q>=6 already follows from the budget, so those cases are fully excluded. For m=2, q=7 by budget and routing is OR-only. Do not infer the entire no-repeat three-pair footprint from the cases above until the fifth-term exception is closed.
