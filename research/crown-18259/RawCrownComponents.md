# Necessary raw Crown sharing constraints

For a monotone extension of the promised one-left/one-right Crown table, the empty and single-primary assignments are false (each is below a false diagonal). Fix all other primaries to zero: opposite-side inputs x_i,y_j with i!=j have the AND truth table. Fix a different diagonal x_k=y_k=1 instead, with k distinct from i and j and other inputs zero. Neither added input gives false, either one gives a true off-diagonal by monotonicity, so the same pair has an OR cofactor. The generic prerequisites and statements compile in CrownComponentWitnesses.lean.

A live minimum circuit can be cut at every internal gate with multiple consumers, leaving formula components with named shared signals as inputs. A primary with one consumer appears in exactly one component. The output of that component is the only source of dependence on that primary in later components. Two unique inputs in one formula cannot exhibit both an AND and OR cofactor in different restrictions: after fixing all other signals, if both inputs are retained, their fixed least-common-ancestor connective remains. The downstream one-bit continuation is monotone, hence either the identity or constant. Therefore no component can contain two opposite-side unique primaries from different indices.

Count an index as started once either primary is repeated (precisely, has multiple readers) or appears in the current or an earlier component in topological order. A component cannot start two distinct indices. If it did, two unique start primaries must be on the same side as above, so the component cannot also have an opposite-side unique primary. Both of their twins must be in later components. Fix repeated primaries, earlier-primary inputs, and the other unique primaries of the current component to zero. The two starts can be activated individually or both absent. Among the two free twin positions on the opposite side, these three settings give the two complementary row signatures and an all-false signature (column version by transposition). The current formula root is a bit depending on only the two starts, so the rest of the circuit cannot realize three distinct response functions. Contradiction. The same argument excludes two starts in the final component, where the twins have no later components.

If h is the number of shared internal nodes and d the number of indices with at least one shared primary, this gives h+d >= n-1. Formula-component labels also enforce the pairwise constraints above. The count does not exclude 22 gates for n=7; it is a lower structural constraint for exact raw synthesis. The Lean artifact verifies the cofactor ingredients, not the whole reduction.

A separate local circuit experiment uses crown-local-derivative.cpp and crown-rewire-seeds.txt (five true 23-gate controls). With a C++17 compiler, run crown-local-derivative <seedfile> 42 0 to replay all 2,695 internal-deletion/redirect starts, every subsequent single gate replacement, and one further replacement from selected score-one starts. No witness. An optional time budget greater than zero performs randomized coordinate descent, which cannot establish a minimum. The closest tested 22-gate circuits get 48 of the original 49 ordered inputs correct.
