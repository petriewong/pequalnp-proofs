# Raw Crown 7: four pure cuts

Research archive, not an upstream submission. The four-cut form is the OR of four ANDs, each reading two pure OR unions over the original fourteen primaries. At 22 gates there are at most 15 union gates. Four partial bits encode the side of each cut where a raw input occurs. Matched row/column subcubes intersect; each unmatched row/column pair must be disjoint. Pick a common representative for each of the seven matched pairs and quotient the representative set under cube symmetries. The included 56 sets cover all 11,440 full representative sets. Candidate raw subcubes must contain their own representative and avoid all others, and are checked for the exact cross-disjointness. Identical raw incidence classes are premerged at one OR per merge; this is exact for pure OR unions by clearing one twin and collapsing a consuming OR before restoring their paired OR.

Compile `PureRawFour.cpp` with C++17 and run its binary with `four-cube-seeds.txt 15`. The sharded version takes the seed file, budget, shard index and shard count. Verified finite search covered 56 representatives and 2,670,252,741 candidate label layouts, counting duplicates from different representative choices; no solution at the budget. This is not a general lower bound for Crown with mixed or more than four ANDs and is not a fully Lean-encoded computation. `Crown23RawFour.lean` is the checked 23-gate positive control. `CutSubcubes.lean` proves the representative reduction; `CleanErase.lean` separately proves semantic preservation when erasing earlier clean products from operand traces, not physical gate accounting.
