# Excluding the two-pair, all-sole-OR case at 22 gates

Consider a useful monotone fan-in-two AND/OR circuit correct on the 49 Crown7 promises. Type O is a split raw input with a sole OR reader. The two inputs of a pure matched OR feed only that gate. We prove (i) no terminalized circuit of at most 21 gates has exactly two pure matched ORs, and (ii) no circuit of at most 22 gates with exactly two pure matched ORs has all ten split raws of type O. The three-or-more-pair exclusion is used to handle any new pairing created by simplification. The size-22 case with exceptional raw readers is not settled here.

## A 21-gate cut budget with two pure pairs

Set one split raw on each side to zero at differing labels, then identify each twin pair. The restricted function is exact seven-variable threshold two; its gate lower bound is 16 and its OR lower bound is 10. The two pure gates collapse, independently of outside-prefix losses. Hence at size at most 21, any such cut can lose at most three outside-prefix gates.

The individual elementary zero-shadow is obtained by propagating zeros, simplifying any resulting constant or unary gate. It has at most two gates. Indeed, if it had three or more, the cut budget would force exactly three and every gate reading any of the four compatible opposite split raws would already be in those three. Each shadow gate has at least one input supplied by the original zero raw or its zero propagation, leaving at most one slot for these four distinct other raws. This is impossible. The clash only uses gates in the original DAG: alias substitution never adds a slot.

A sole AND reader always forces a shadow beyond itself, since its output cannot be final (other promised positives exist). It must have exactly one consumer, an OR, and its shadow consists of these two gates. If a raw has two readers, both must be ORs: an AND reader can only fit if its sole consumer is the other direct reader, which would be OR(v, AND(v,s)), redundant. By the same shadow bound, these are all the cases O, A, R. The masking arguments of CrownRepeatReaders.md depend only on the three-loss outside-prefix budget: mismatched opposite A-A and R-A cannot coexist, R-R share precisely one OR, and O and an opposite mismatched exceptional raw have disjoint shadows. Any cut involving an exceptional raw therefore saturates the budget.

## Terminalized circuits at size at most 21

A clean signal rejects all matched diagonals. Terminalized means no nonzero clean signal is consumed in the routing; useful clean outputs originate at product ANDs and may be combined by terminal ORs. If both O and exceptional raws occur, there is an O label distinct from an exceptional label: if a fixed exceptional raw shares a label with every O, then all four other labels are entirely exceptional. Pick such a distinct pair of labels. Choose an opposite cut away from the O label, through that exceptional raw and any opposite split raw of yet another label. It saturates the budget. The O feeder survives this cut since sharing a mismatched raw input or serving as a mismatched A downstream OR would mask a required pivot. Its companion cannot accept a different diagonal (also by masking), or its own (the feeder would alias its companion on the tied cube for one extra loss). This requires a clean companion, impossible in terminalized routing. The leaf raw itself cannot feed a terminal OR because it would accept a diagonal.

If all raws are exceptional and an R exists, the four mismatched opposite raws must all be R and share a distinct gate with it, requiring four raw readers rather than two. If there is no R, mismatched A-A is impossible. It remains to exclude all-O at this size; the following argument also deals with size 22.

## All split raws have a sole OR reader

The ten O feeders are distinct. A sole shared OR of different labels masks a diagonal pivot; a sole shared OR within one label would make a third pure pair. Each opposite cut deletes two distinct O feeders and the two pure ORs, leaving at least 10 ORs after projection. Thus there are at least 14 original ORs, so at most seven ANDs at size 21 or eight at size 22.

Each feeder companion rejects all diagonals except possibly its own. At most one feeder per label can have a companion true at its own diagonal. In fact, such a companion must depend on the twin (otherwise it would be true at zero or at the feeder's sole raw singleton by a path that precedes the sole reader). It must therefore occur after the twin's sole reader; both feeders cannot precede each other. If there are two different own-dirty labels at size 21, choose a cut away from these two labels (among the other three split labels). Both kept feeders alias their own companions in the projection, on top of four distinct cut losses, contradicting the allowance of five. At size 22, three different own-dirty labels are excluded by cutting across the other two split labels, resulting in at least seven losses when only six are allowed. Alias losses are counted by the distinct feeder gates, which survive raw elimination; simultaneous alias substitutions are acyclic.

Erase internal reads of original clean signals and collect the productive original clean ANDs at a new terminal OR tree (the clean erasure argument in CrownNoThreePairs.md). Each clean-companion feeder aliases its raw, and at most A-1 new ORs are needed if there were A original ANDs. The result retains both original pure conduits because both raw inputs of each are essential and occur nowhere else. At size at most 21, nine or more removed feeders and at most six new ORs give at most 18 gates. If an additional pure pair formed it would contradict the earlier exclusion for three or more at ≤22. Otherwise an opposite cut through the remaining split labels loses at least four gates (besides the pure prefix, there are two distinct raw-reading gates: if both raws had only the same gate, a shared AND would erase each raw’s influence on its positives with other inputs, while a shared OR would mask the pivot from a diagonal to a superset containing a promised positive). This requires at least 20 gates, also a contradiction. This completes (i). At size at most 22, eight or more removed feeders and at most seven new ORs give at most 21 terminalized gates; (i) and the three-or-more-pair exclusion finish (ii).
