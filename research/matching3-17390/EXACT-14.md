# 3×3 perfect matching needs 14 binary monotone gates (computational proof)

For the row-major 9-edge Boolean function, the attached Lean witness computes it on every assignment in 14 binary AND/OR gates. The lower bound is a finite computational argument. It is not a formal Lean proof of optimality and does not supply the upstream positive existence statement for 13 gates.

See LOWER-BOUND.md for all sizes at most 12 and the arguments excluding zero or one shared internal gate up through 13. For k=13, total fanout excess is five. The first gate forces at least two primary edges to be shared. The only possible (shared raw, shared internal) counts are (2,2), (2,3), and (3,2); in the last case every shared node has fanout two. In the first two cases the two shared primaries are the first operation's inputs; their positions are equivalent to 0,1 or 0,4, and the operation may be either AND or OR. These eight cases are unsatisfiable.

In the three-shared-primary case fix the first pair as above, then pick the third representative by the stabilizer of the first pair (row/column permutations, transpose and first-pair commutativity). Representatives: 2,3,5 for pair 0,1; 1,2,8 for pair 0,4. There are 12 cases with the operation. Four general models and five models with full raw-pair pivot guards return unsatisfiable directly. The other three reduce to these two primary pools: {0,1,2} and {0,4,8}. For every primary in either pool, with the other two taking either Boolean value, an exhaustive 512-row check finds a corresponding pivot (switching the primary from zero to one changes the target from false to true). A raw primary with two readers cannot spend both readers on different raw pairs: one of those pivot rows shields both gates. If two raw-pair gates read the same pair, either their operation is identical and one is redundant, or the two gates compute AND and OR and make those two inputs interchangeable. Matching is not invariant under a transposition of a single edge pair. Thus only the first gate reads two raw variables. The remaining ten raw incidences and twelve gates require exactly ten gates with one raw input and two gates with only internal inputs. For each of the three remaining cases, the two possible sorts of root are both UNSAT.

Run sh replay_size13.sh in this directory with Python and z3-solver installed. The replay expects explicit UNSAT per case. A timeout is a failed replay, not a negative answer. To independently verify full truth on the upper witness, run python3 check_matching.py or check PerfectMatching14.lean.
